## Project Overview
Tshepo ya Sechaba (TSO) is a non-profit organisation based in Dawn Park and Vosloorus. It supports the elderly, toddlers (ages 2-3) and women in need, and is funded solely by donations. This website raises awareness of TSO's programmes, attracts donors and sponsors, and recruits volunteers.

## Website Goals and Objectives
- Raise awareness of TSO's programmes for the elderly and vulnerable.
- Attract and convert donors and investors.
- Widen TSO's networks and recruit volunteers.
- Success indicators (KPIs): <e.g. number of enquiry submissions, volunteer sign-ups, monthly visitors; copy these from your proposal>

## Key Features and Functionality
- Five-page website: Home, About Us, Services, Enquiry, Contact Us
- Responsive layout for desktop, tablet and mobile
- About page: accordion and image gallery with lightbox
- Services page: searchable and filterable programme list
- Enquiry form with validation and an instant response (volunteer or sponsor)
- Contact form with validation that builds an email to TSO
- Interactive Leaflet map showing two locations
